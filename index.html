        Listening check

        Answers by ear whether the BTR15 widens peaking filters. The page puts one strong cut on the BTR15 (live, not saved) and plays pink noise through the exact inverse of what each model predicts. The right model cancels the cut and sounds like the plain-noise reference; the wrong one leaves an audible colouration.

        1. In Windows sound settings, make the BTR15 the output device. Turn off other EQ and sound effects (phone EQ, Windows enhancements).

        3. Switch between R, A and B. Which of A or B sounds exactly like R?
